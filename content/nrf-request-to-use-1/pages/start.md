# Request to use the nature restoration levy

Use this service when you are ready to start the planning and development process and want to use the nature restoration levy to meet the environmental obligations covered by an [Environmental Delivery Plan (EDP)](#).

Once your request is accepted, you will receive a commitment certificate. You can submit this alongside your planning application.

:::button Start now
:::

## Before you start

You will need the NRF reference from your quote email. If you do not have a quote yet, [get a quote for the nature restoration levy](/nrf-quote-7/start) first.

## What happens after you request to use the levy

Natural England will check your request against the quote and the EDP. You do not need to pay anything now.
