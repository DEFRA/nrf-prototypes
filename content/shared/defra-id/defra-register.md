# You need to register for a Defra account

To access Defra services you need to have a Defra account.

Once you have registered you can access and manage Defra services:

- for yourself as a business or individual
- on behalf of businesses that you represent

:::inset
You'll use your Government Gateway details to sign in to Defra services each time you visit.
:::

To find out how we use, process and store your personal information, read our [Privacy notice (opens in new tab)](#).
